About  

I'm a postdoctoral researcher at TRIUMF, interested in nuclear theory and the quantum many body problem.

Below you can find more information about me: my CV, the links to my publications, and where you can currently find me.

I recently taught a crash course on ab initio nuclear theory at the University of Barcelona.
